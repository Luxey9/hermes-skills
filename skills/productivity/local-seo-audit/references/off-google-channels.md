# Off-Google channels for a local service business

Google (Maps + organic) is the primary funnel, but the owner will ask about
Instagram and Facebook. Answer with a channel decision, then hand over post-ready
material — advice alone changes nothing.

## Facebook beats Instagram for this class of business

Not because of audience size, but because of *who is asking*.

| | Facebook | Instagram |
|---|---|---|
| Local groups ("warga <kecamatan>") | the core lever — tens of thousands of members, people **ask** and get a number back | no equivalent |
| Intent | solving a problem now, recommending | browsing |
| Homeowner age band | 30–55, many own the house | 18–30, many rent or live with parents |
| Local spread | share + recommendation chains | needs followers first |

Order the channels: **Maps + reviews → Facebook page and 2–3 local groups →
Instagram (trust, slow) → TikTok (optional)**. Say plainly that IF the owner has
limited energy, only the first two should be worked now.

## Content mix — refuse the "explain what electricity is" plan

Owners often propose educational content ("apa itu listrik dan jenis-jenisnya").
Redirect it: nobody searches for a physics lesson, and a service purchase is
triggered by **proof**, not understanding. Give the ratio instead:

- **40% proof of work** — before/after photos, messy panel to labelled panel,
  charred MCB replaced. A 15-second clip shot at job completion; do not wait for
  a perfect shot.
- **30% self-diagnosable symptoms** — "MCB trips every time the AC starts: three
  causes", "tingling at the tap means the earthing is failing". This is what gets
  saved and re-found on a phone at 11 p.m.
- **20% real incidents** — the 2 a.m. call, the panel from a 1990s house. This is
  what makes the account feel alive.
- **10% team, tools, coverage.**

Rules: no motivational quotes, no obviously AI-generated job photos (instant
credibility loss for a trade), and the bio carries district + service + number
rather than "jasa terpercaya". Measure clicks to WhatsApp, not followers; if two
months produce zero calls, move the energy to reviews instead of posting harder.

## Group posting that does not get deleted

Local groups are not billboards. Lead with the giveaway, put the number last.

- **Maximum one post per group per week.** More reads as spam and gets the account
  flagged by admins.
- **Comment first, sell second** when someone asks "anyone know an electrician?":
  answer the symptom in two lines, then offer the number. That comment outperforms
  any standalone post.
- **The free-help post is the strongest of the set** — "ask me anything about your
  house wiring, no need to hire me" generates more inbound chats than the offer
  post, because the reader was helped before being sold to.
- Write in local plain Indonesian: name the symptom, name the district, skip
  "murah / terpercaya / berpengalaman" stacked as adjectives.
- Phone numbers are not tappable in a post body — write them as `wa.me/<intl>`.
- Log every post: date, group, type, and **how many chats came in**. Without that
  column the owner cannot tell which post type to repeat.

## What you can and cannot verify from outside

Group member counts, group rules, and whether an account is trusted are not
reachable without logging in. Hand over the groups you *can* name from a public
search, and ask the owner to search "warga <kecamatan>" / "jual beli <kecamatan>"
for the rest — do not present guessed membership numbers as fact.
