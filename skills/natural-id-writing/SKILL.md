# Natural Indonesian Writing

## When to Use

- Filling Indonesian forms, commitment sheets, or admin documents for the user.
- Rewriting AI-sounding Indonesian prose into the user's plain voice.

Write Indonesian admin and form text the way the user writes: short sentences, plain words, first person, no AI tells.

## Rules

- Never use em dashes, en dashes, or semicolons in filled prose. A period plus a new short sentence always beats a dash-joined clause.
- One idea per sentence. If a sentence has two clauses joined by a dash or semicolon, split it.
- Prefer concrete verbs over abstract nouns: "saya bagi tugas ke tiap divisi" beats "melakukan pembagian tugas berdasarkan divisi".
- Keep form labels and headers untouched; only the filled content gets naturalized.
- After filling a .docx, sweep the full text for U+2014 and U+2013 and confirm zero remain before delivering.
